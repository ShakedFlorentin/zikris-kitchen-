# zikris-kitchen-

Landing page prototype for **Zikry Kitchens · זיקרי מטבחים**, a family carpentry shop making custom kitchens and interior woodwork.

## Design concept: "כוריאוגרפיה של עץ" (choreography of wood)

The page performs like a dance:

- **Hero, the stage:** three kitchens in stage-arch frames sway like dancers, a brass ribbon moves through them, stage-light beams swing slowly, and a follow-spot tracks the pointer.
- **Tempo:** two crossing marquee bands run in opposite directions (measure, plan, build, install).
- **Work, the chorus line:** projects stand in a staggered line of arches. Style filters move a springy brass pill, and cards step in on their count.
- **Process, four counts:** a wave draws itself as you scroll and lights up each step.
- **Applause:** the customer testimonial.
- **The next step:** contact, phone, WhatsApp and a lead form.

Hebrew and RTL throughout. The accessibility menu has larger text, high contrast, a readable font, **stop animations** and link highlighting. `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole page, with inline CSS and JS and no build step.
- `assets/`: logo and project photos.

Deploy by dragging the folder onto Netlify, or open `index.html` locally.
